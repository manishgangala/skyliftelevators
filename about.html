About Skylift Elevators

Built on-site experience, run like a family business.

Skylift Elevators is a Hyderabad-based lift manufacturing and installation company, proprietor G. Kiran Kumar Goud, working directly with builders and homeowners across the city.

Our story

Started to solve a simple problem: buildings needed lifts they could actually rely on

Skylift Elevators was founded to bring manufacturing and installation of lifts under one roof, so there's a single team responsible for a lift from the first site measurement through years of servicing afterward — rather than a manufacturer, an installer and a service agent who've never spoken to each other.

The company works across home lifts, passenger lifts, hospital lifts and goods lifts, taking on projects sized to what a building — and its budget — actually needs.

What "Grown By Technology" means to us

It's the line on our card, and it's a working principle: every lift we build uses tested components and current safety standards, but the relationship stays personal — the same team that quotes your project installs it and services it.

What guides the work

Three things we don't compromise on

Safety first

Every installation is load-tested and safety-checked before handover — no shortcuts on the parts that matter most.

Straight quotes

The price you're quoted covers the specification you asked for — clearly written, nothing added later.

Long-term service

We stay reachable after installation — maintenance, part replacements and breakdown calls handled by people who know your lift.
