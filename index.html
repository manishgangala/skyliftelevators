What we build

Lifts manufactured and installed for every kind of building

From a compact home elevator to a full-capacity goods lift, each installation is designed around the shaft you have, the load you need to move, and the budget you're working with.

Home Lifts

Space-saving elevators built into duplexes and villas, sized to fit an existing stairwell or a new shaft.

Passenger Lifts

Apartment and office elevators built for daily high-frequency use, with smooth ride quality as standard.

Hospital Lifts

Wide-door, stretcher-friendly cabins engineered for clinics and nursing homes.

Goods & Cargo Lifts

Heavy-duty lifts for warehouses, kitchens and workshops, built to your load and shaft dimensions.

Modernisation

Upgrading older lifts already installed in your building — controllers, cabins and safety systems.

Annual Maintenance

Scheduled servicing plans to keep every lift we install running safely, year after year.

How a project runs

From first call to a lift that's running

1

Site visit & measurement

We visit your building, check the available shaft or wall space, and understand what the lift needs to carry.

2

Design & quotation

You get a written quote with cabin size, load capacity, speed and a clear price — no hidden add-ons later.

3

Manufacturing

Components are manufactured and checked against the agreed specification before they leave for site.

4

Installation & testing

Our team installs the lift on site and runs full safety and load testing before handover.

5

After-sales service

Ongoing maintenance and a direct line to the team that built your lift, not a call centre.
